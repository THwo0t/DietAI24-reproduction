# P development science freeze

`freeze_p.py` runs offline, accepts no source/configuration overrides, and reads
the real complete cohort/runtime/scoring review gate first. The coordinator
creates that gate only after the single independent integrated review and
offline confirmation of every required fix. Neither this entry nor preparation
creates review approval. Missing/pending gates refuse before prepared inputs.

The freeze binds exact prepared roles, copied development images, both catalogs,
all manifest-declared official vectors, model/environment/parameters, source and
parser modules, preparation evidence, review records, and scoring policy. Every
preparation review source remains exact. Large raw archives and full mechanical
receipts remain outside runtime source hashing; their authenticated digests are
retained in the copied source proof. Labels are hashed as opaque bytes here and
are never parsed. Inference calibration remains disabled and fold medians remain
the immutable preparation outputs.

The fixed blind population determines all reachable request slots and a frozen
three-attempt transport cap. Sources are hashed again before exclusive private
publication, and the complete runtime context must then validate the resulting
freeze. Sources, environment, review and exact freeze bytes are checked again
after that validation before a success claim. Exact-byte resume is allowed; conflicting/partial output is retained
and refused. A failed final context check cannot authorize inference. No model
call, metric selection, independent acceptance designation or download occurs.

The integrated review also covers the separate postscore gate and its source
inventory. The freeze includes that gate and review, avoiding a self-hash cycle.
The completed-run zero-provider verifier must authenticate all scientific and
service artifacts before postscore semantically accesses development labels.
Any later method/code/source change requires a new registered phase, rather than
editing an existing inference freeze or selecting against old acceptance data.
